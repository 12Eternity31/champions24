The story

seoul, 2024
“First LAN international title for China. First MVP trophy for a Chinese player. One map five to make it real.” VALORANT CHAMPIONS 2024 · GRAND FINAL

Edward Gaming did not arrive in Seoul as favourites. They arrived as the team that kept losing the close ones — the roster that could out-aim anyone on a good day and then lose a map to a single retake. The grand final started exactly like that: Haven, 6–13, nine straight rounds handed to Team Heretics while EDG's executes died on a Cypher setup.

What changed was not the aim. It was the pace. On Sunset, EDG opened 9–0 and never let the map breathe — a 13–4 answer built on seven first kills from ZmjjKK. Lotus was the grind: 6–6 at the half, then four of the last five rounds to go up 2–1.

Bind was Heretics' reply. MiniBoo found his form again — 23 kills, a 12–8 lead, and a Brimstone molly line that denied EDG's defuse at the death. The series went to Abyss.

EDG built 8–4 at the half by out-aiming, stretched it to 11–4, and then Heretics won five straight to make it 11–9. The next round was the series: a Kangkang retake that removed the momentum entirely. EDG closed it 13–9.

Why it matters

China's first international VALORANT title, and the first Champions MVP awarded to a Chinese player.
